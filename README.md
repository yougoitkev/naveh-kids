# Crafted Wonders

Create a premium, modern e-commerce website for a handcrafted wooden products business.

Brand & Business

The business creates handcrafted wooden products, especially children's wooden toys and ride-on products. Products are made from natural wood, designed to be durable, safe, visually beautiful, and easy to assemble.

Example products:

Wooden rocking horses

Wooden ride-on horses

Wooden scooters

Wooden balance toys

Wooden cars

Wooden bikes

Wooden educational toys

Wooden furniture and accessories

Other handcrafted wooden products

The brand should feel premium, warm, trustworthy, handcrafted, minimal, and modern.

The website should NOT look like a generic toy store. It should feel like a premium design-focused wooden products brand.

Design Direction

Take inspiration from the design philosophy of Apple and Google:

Extremely clean layouts

Generous whitespace

Strong typography

Beautiful product photography

Minimal visual clutter

Clear hierarchy

Smooth transitions

Subtle animations

Premium but approachable

Excellent mobile experience

Do NOT copy Apple or Google branding, logos, layouts, or proprietary design elements. Use their general principles of simplicity, clarity, and polished interaction design.

The visual identity should combine:

Natural wood + modern technology + premium children's products.

Use a warm neutral palette:

Natural wood tones

Warm off-white / ivory backgrounds

Soft beige

Dark charcoal text

Subtle earthy accent colours

Avoid overly bright toy-store colours.
